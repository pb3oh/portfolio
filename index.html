§ 01

My AI-Native
Principles

A working philosophy formed shipping production AI to millions of customers and tens of thousands of financial experts.

01

Agentic workflows over monolithic prompts.

Real work decomposes into roles, tools, and handoffs. I design systems where specialized agents plan, retrieve, act, and verify — with deterministic checkpoints between the probabilistic steps.

02

Governance-by-design, not as an audit afterthought.

Eval harnesses, policy gates, lineage, and red-team coverage live in the same repo as the agent graph. If it can't be measured and attested, it doesn't ship to regulated customers.

03

Human-centric orchestration. The expert stays in the loop.

The most valuable AI surfaces don't replace the professional — they compound them. I build for the CPA, the analyst, the underwriter: confidence over autonomy, traceability over magic.
